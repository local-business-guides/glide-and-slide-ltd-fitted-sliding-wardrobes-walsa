Start your alcove wardrobe near me search with a storage list

Before choosing a finish, take stock of what the wardrobe needs to hold. Long coats need a different arrangement from folded sweaters, and a collection of shoes can take up more room than expected. Write down what currently lives elsewhere in the bedroom and what you'd like to bring together.

Think about who uses the storage, too. Shared wardrobes often work better when each person has a defined section. If one person needs hanging rails and the other prefers shelves, matching interiors on both sides may waste useful space.

Measure the alcove without treating it as a perfect box

A quick width measurement is helpful for an early conversation, but it isn't enough to settle the design. Walls can lean, floors can rise toward one corner, and the back of a recess may be narrower than its opening. Skirting boards also affect how components meet the wall.

For your initial notes, record the following:

  • Width near the floor, halfway up, and near the ceiling.
  • Height at both ends of the opening.
  • Depth on each side of the recess.
  • Positions of outlets, switches, vents, and nearby radiators.
  • Space between the proposed wardrobe front and the bed.

Use these as discussion measurements. Leave final sizing to the fitted furniture team before anything is manufactured.

Compare alcove wardrobe layouts before door finishes

Sliding doors don't swing into the room, but their opening position still matters. A drawer behind a door overlap needs enough clearance to pull out. Ask to see how each interior section will be reached when the doors are moved across.

Depth deserves attention as well. Clothes on hangers need room to sit properly behind the doors. If the recess is shallow, ask how far the wardrobe would extend into the bedroom and what that would mean for your walking space.

When comparing results for alcove wardrobe near me, bring these practical questions to the conversation. A finish sample can help you choose a color, but it can't tell you whether your laundry basket will fit below the bottom shelf.

Prepare for your alcove wardrobe consultation

Take a straight-on photo of the recess and another showing the surrounding room. Include the bedroom entrance, nearby furniture, and any ceiling slope. Mention planned changes, such as new flooring, before measurements are finalized because they can affect the finished fit.

Avoid ordering doors first and planning the interior afterward. Both parts need to work together. Also, don't assume a fitted wardrobe will resolve damp patches or cover a vent safely. Raise those issues before the design moves forward so any necessary work can be discussed.
