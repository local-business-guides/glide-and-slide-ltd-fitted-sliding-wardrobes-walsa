Fitted alcove storage planned around daily use in Walsall

The inside deserves as much attention as the doors. Longer garments need suitable hanging clearance, while smaller folded items benefit from shelves that keep them visible. An alcove wardrobe shouldn't leave you reaching past one group of belongings to get to another every morning.

A few useful points to bring to your design discussion include:

  • The types of clothing you'll keep in the wardrobe.
  • Any larger belongings that need dedicated space.
  • Your preference for mirrored or other sliding doors.
  • Parts of the current bedroom layout you want to improve.

Photographs and approximate dimensions can help you explain your ideas. Final fitting details need careful consideration, especially where ceilings slope or the recess has an unusual shape.
