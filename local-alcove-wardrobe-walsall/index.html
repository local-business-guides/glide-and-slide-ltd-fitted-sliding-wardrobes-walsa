Before arranging a local alcove wardrobe Walsall visit

Start by clearing enough of the recess to see its edges, floor and back wall. You don't need to empty the whole bedroom, but boxes and existing furniture can hide details that affect a fitted wardrobe. Take photographs of the opening and a wider view showing the surrounding walls.

For your local alcove wardrobe Walsall enquiry, explain what currently makes the space difficult to use. Perhaps a deep corner collects bags, or a chest of drawers blocks access beside the bed. Those details give us a clearer starting point than a photograph of a door finish alone.

Alcove wardrobe measurements worth bringing

Rough dimensions help start a conversation, but don't treat one width measurement as a complete specification. Measure the opening near the top, middle and bottom, then note the height at both sides. Record the depth too, including anything that projects from the wall.

Keep a simple checklist:

  • Mark sockets, switches and any cables that need access.
  • Note skirting boards, coving and changes in floor level.
  • Show nearby radiators and window coverings in your photographs.
  • Mention sloping ceilings or visible differences between the walls.
  • Explain whether flooring or decorating work is still planned.

Label these as your initial measurements. Ask how dimensions will be checked before manufacturing, especially where the recess narrows or the ceiling changes height.

Planning access inside an alcove wardrobe

A recess can have enough room for storage but still need careful thought about how you'll reach it. With sliding doors, different sections become accessible as the panels move. Ask us to explain which shelves and rails you'll be able to reach through each opening.

Bring a few examples of what needs storing. Long coats, folded bedding and everyday shirts don't need identical spaces. If drawers are part of your proposed interior, ask how their position works with the door openings and surrounding furniture.

For a local alcove wardrobe Walsall project, it's also useful to mention who will use each section. Someone who struggles to reach high shelves may need a different arrangement from someone mainly storing seasonal items.

Alcove fitting mistakes to avoid

Don't cover a socket in your sketch and assume it can simply disappear behind the wardrobe. Point it out and discuss access. Any electrical changes should be handled by an appropriate professional, rather than treated as part of choosing the furniture.

Avoid ordering around unfinished room changes without discussing them first. New flooring, altered skirting or ceiling work may affect the final dimensions. Tell us what will change and ask when the wardrobe should be measured.
