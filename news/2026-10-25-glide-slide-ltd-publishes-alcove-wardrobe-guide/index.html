Glide & Slide Ltd Publishes Alcove Wardrobe Guide

Glide & Slide Ltd has published an alcove wardrobe guide, with related video and audio content for homeowners considering fitted bedroom storage.

By Glide and Slide ltd - Fitted Sliding WardrobesUpdated 2026-10-25Walsall

  • Glide & Slide Ltd published the alcove wardrobe guide and related content.
  • The releases include a website article, YouTube video, podcast episode and SoundCloud audio.
  • The information is for homeowners considering fitted bedroom storage.
  • The article is on the company's website; the video is on YouTube and audio is on SoundCloud.
  • A free design consultation can be requested; no phone number was provided.

WALSALL (October 25, 2026) Glide & Slide Ltd has published a guide to alcove wardrobes, alongside a video on YouTube, a podcast episode and an audio version on SoundCloud. The company has also published an article on its website covering the same topic.

The guide is for homeowners in Walsall and across the West Midlands who are considering bedroom storage fitted to their room. It focuses on alcove wardrobes and the practical question of making storage work in a space where standard furniture may not fit. Readers can explore how a made-to-measure wardrobe could be planned for an alcove, as well as how fitted storage can suit sloping ceilings and other awkward layouts.

The material gives homeowners a starting point as they think about bedroom storage and the shape of their own room. A fitted solution can be tailored to the available space, rather than relying on standard furniture dimensions. The video and audio options also let people access the topic in different formats.

The article is available on Glide & Slide Ltd’s website. The video can be watched on YouTube, and the audio version is on SoundCloud. The company has also released a podcast episode on alcove wardrobes. Homeowners interested in a made-to-measure solution can request a free design consultation from Glide & Slide Ltd. No phone number was included in the information supplied for this release.

About Glide and Slide ltd - Fitted Sliding Wardrobes

Glide & Slide Ltd specialises in bespoke fitted sliding wardrobes, mirrored wardrobes and custom bedroom storage across the West Midlands and surrounding areas. The company has over 30 years of experience designing, manufacturing and installing wardrobes tailored to alcoves, sloping ceilings and awkward layouts.
