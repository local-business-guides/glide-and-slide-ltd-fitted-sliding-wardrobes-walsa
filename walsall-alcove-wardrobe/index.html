An uneven wall or nearby socket can change a wardrobe plan. Here's what to record before your consultation and which fitting details need a professional assessment.

Getting your walsall alcove wardrobe project started

Take a clear photograph of the whole wall before focusing on the recess. Include the ceiling, floor and furniture on either side. A close-up might show the alcove itself but miss a radiator, bedroom doorway or ceiling feature that affects the installation.

For a walsall alcove wardrobe, early notes are useful for explaining the job, but they shouldn't be treated as final manufacturing measurements. Walls can lean, floors can slope and an opening can vary from top to bottom. Those differences need checking before fitted components are made.

Recording your Walsall alcove before a consultation

If you can measure safely, note the approximate width at several heights and the height at each side. Record the recess depth too. Label your notes so it's clear which measurement belongs to which part of the opening.

You don't need to remove trim or lift flooring to gather initial information. Photograph visible details and mention anything you can't check. Guessing what's behind a boxed section or above a ceiling panel can create confusion later.

Prepare the following information for the discussion:

  • Photos showing skirting boards and other projecting trim.
  • Positions of sockets, switches and visible cables.
  • Details of nearby radiators, pipes or vents.
  • Any planned changes to flooring or decoration.
  • Whether existing furniture needs removing.
  • The route from the entrance to the bedroom.

This gives the conversation a clear starting point and helps identify questions that require an on-site look.

Spotting alcove wardrobe issues before fitting

Mention damp patches, staining or loose plaster before discussing installation dates. A wardrobe won't resolve an underlying wall problem. If the cause is unknown, have it assessed by an appropriate professional before enclosing that part of the room.

Sockets and switches also deserve attention. Tell the designer if you still need to use them, and ask how the proposed wardrobe affects access. Any necessary electrical changes should be discussed with a qualified electrician rather than assumed to be part of the furniture work.

If new flooring is planned, explain that early. Ask which order of work suits the proposed installation, since changes to floor level or trim can affect the fit. Avoid ordering against measurements taken before an unresolved room alteration.

Moving your walsall alcove wardrobe toward installation

Before work begins, agree how the bedroom should be prepared. Ask how much space needs clearing, where components will be brought through and what access the installer needs. Keep the stairs and landing in the discussion if the bedroom is upstairs.

Check any uncertainty while the plan can still be adjusted. A sloping ceiling, irregular corner or obstruction is a reason to request professional measuring rather than trying to correct dimensions yourself.

Questions people ask about walsall alcove wardrobe

Can I provide my own alcove measurements?

Approximate dimensions can help explain the space during an initial conversation. Ask for professional measuring before components are manufactured, especially where surfaces aren't straight or level.

What if there's a socket inside the alcove?

Show its position and explain whether you need continued access to it. Discuss any required relocation with a qualified electrician before the wardrobe layout is finalized.

Should I decorate before the alcove wardrobe is installed?

Tell the designer what decorating and flooring work you have planned. Agree on the order before starting so the room preparation matches the installation requirements.
